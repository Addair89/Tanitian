Getting there

Nearly all visitors arrive by air. Taniti's airport currently handles small jets and propeller planes; an expansion now underway will let larger jets land within the next few years, so flight options should widen over time.

A small cruise ship also calls at Yellow Leaf Bay one night a week, for travelers arriving by sea.

Money & power

Currency US dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen, and major credit cards are widely taken.
Currency exchange Available at several banks around Taniti City.
Power outlets 120 volts — the same as the United States, so no adapter needed if you're traveling from the US.

Customs & laws

Alcohol hours Not served or sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m.
Drinking age 18, though it isn't strictly enforced.
Language Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents — a translation app or phrasebook helps outside Taniti City.

Health & safety

Taniti has one hospital and several smaller clinics; the hospital has many multilingual staff on hand. See the emergency numbers page for direct contacts and clinic locations by island.

Violent crime is very rare. As tourism has grown, reports of pickpocketing and other petty theft have increased slightly — the usual precautions (secure your bag, don't flash valuables) apply, especially around busy beaches and markets.

Holidays & hours

Taniti observes a large number of national holidays. Many restaurants and attractions close for the day, so it's worth checking a date against the local holiday calendar before building it into your itinerary.
