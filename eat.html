What's on the menu

Taniti is a small island, so the dining scene is compact — ten restaurants in total, plus a handful of grocery stores if you'd rather cook for yourselves.

10 restaurants island-wide

Mostly local, with options

Half of Taniti's restaurants serve traditional Tanitian fish-and-rice dishes. The rest cover familiar American-style meals and Pan-Asian cuisine.

Grocery & convenience

If you're staying somewhere with a kitchen, or just want snacks on hand, Taniti has a small but reliable grocery scene.

Supermarkets

Two full-size supermarkets in Taniti City carry produce, meat and household basics.
