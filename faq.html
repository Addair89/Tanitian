Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most before and during a trip to Taniti.

Booking & packages Activities Travel & entry Families

Booking & packages

Can I change or cancel a package after booking?

Yes. Packages can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before your first activity. Within 48 hours, a 50% fee applies. Date changes are free, subject to availability.

Do package prices include flights?

No. All prices shown on the Packages page are per person for activities, meals and ground transfers only. Flights to Taniti are booked separately.

Can I book individual activities instead of a package?

Yes. Every listing on the Things to do page can be booked on its own, priced per person, with no package required.

Activities

How is the difficulty rating decided?

Ratings reflect physical effort and terrain: one dot is suitable for most fitness levels, two involves sustained walking or swimming, three involves elevation, heat or exposure and requires a reasonable fitness level.

What happens if the Volcano trek is cancelled for safety reasons?

Vent Peak is monitored daily. If a trek is cancelled for volcanic activity or weather, you'll be offered a different activity of equal value or a full refund.

Travel & entry

Do I need a visa to visit Taniti?

Most visitors receive a free 30-day entry permit on arrival. Check the Transportation page for the full list of documents to bring.

How do I get between islands?

Passenger ferries run several times a day between the four main islands. Details and timetables are on the Transportation page.

Families

Which activities work for young children?

Beaches, Fishing and Guided tours are marked "All ages." Snorkeling is suitable from age 6, Rainforest hike from 8, Ziplining from 10. Vent Peak trek is recommended for 14 and up only.

Is medical care available on the islands?

Each main island has a clinic; the main hospital is on the capital island. See the Emergency numbers page for contact details.
