Getting around Taniti

How to arrive, how to island-hop, and how to get to your activities once you're here.

Arriving Inter-island ferries On the island

Arriving in Taniti

All international flights land at Taniti International Airport (TNT) on Capital Island. Most visitors receive a free 30-day entry permit on arrival — bring a passport valid for at least 6 months and proof of onward travel.

Airport transfers are included in every package. Booking activities individually? Taxis queue outside arrivals, roughly $18–25 USD to central Capital Island.

Inter-island ferries

Passenger ferries connect the four main islands. Buy tickets at any harbor kiosk or online up to a day in advance during peak season.

Route Duration Frequency Fare
Capital → Reef Island 35 min Every 2 hours, 6am–7pm $12
Capital → Rainforest Island 55 min 4x daily $16
Capital → South Island 70 min 3x daily $18
Reef → Rainforest Island 40 min 2x daily $14

On the island

Rental cars — Available at the airport and in Capital Island town. An international driving permit is required. Roads are paved on the coast, unpaved inland.

Taxis — Metered in town, flat-rate for longer trips. Agree the fare before you get in outside Capital Island.

Activity pickups — Fishing, Ziplining and Volcano trek include a hotel pickup in the price. Snorkeling, Rainforest hike and Guided tours meet at a fixed harbor or trailhead point, shown on your booking confirmation.

Walking — Beach towns are walkable. The rainforest interior and Vent Peak require a guide; independent hiking off marked trails isn't permitted.
